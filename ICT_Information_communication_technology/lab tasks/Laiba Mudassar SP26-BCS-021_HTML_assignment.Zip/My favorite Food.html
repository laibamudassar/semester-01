My favorite Food: Chapli Kabab

Chapli Kabab is a famous patty-shaped minced meat kabab, originating from Peshawar. It is made from ground beef or mutton with various spices and is unique because of its large, flat shape. The kabab is fried in animal fat or oil, giving it a crispy exterior while remaining juicy on the inside. It is best served hot with fresh naan and yogurt chutney.I Love this dish because it has a rich flavor and represents the delicious food culture of pakistan.it remains a symbol of the rich culinary heritage of Pakistan.


Ingredients

  1. Minced Beef or Mutton
  2. Crushed Pomegranate Seeds (Anardana)
  3. Maize Flour (Makai ka Atta)
  4. Fresh Tomatoes and Green Chilies
  5. Scrambled Eggs (mixed in the dough)
